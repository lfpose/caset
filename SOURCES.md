# Sources

Every recording on caset.cl, where it came from, and its license as stated by the source. Tracks marked `permission` are not deployed; they live in the gitignored `held/` folder until cleared. Where a licence requires attribution, or the source asks for credit, the track carries a `credit` line (and a `license_url` for Creative Commons licences) in `public/tapes.json`, shown on the liner card under the track.
